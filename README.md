תעשה בסוף שיצא לי pdf , עם הפרטים שמלאתי , 







Add a spell search UI that lets me search spells and includes spells/abilities granted by my subclasses and chosen feats.







Add an items step that lets me choose items from wikidot and apply their bonuses (AC, HP, attacks, ability mods, etc.) to the final stats.







Calculate how far my character can walk based on current movement speed (including bonuses) and show it on the character page and PDF.







Add Fates (feat/talent) options to my character and apply their bonuses to the character sheet calculations.
